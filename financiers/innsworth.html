Litigation Funder Profile

Innsworth

UK · Funded by Elliott Management

Funds commercial litigation and arbitration claims above roughly $100M, including shareholder and class actions, insolvency claims, and investor-state arbitration. Formerly Bentham Europe, a joint venture of IMF Bentham and Elliott Management.

Investment criteria: Minimum claim size roughly $100M, one of the highest thresholds in the market. Shareholder and class actions, insolvency claims, and investor-state arbitration only.
