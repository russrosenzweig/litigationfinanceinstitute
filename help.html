If you or someone else is in danger right now, do not wait for this website.

In the United States, call 911 in an emergency. For suicide or emotional crisis support, call or text 988.

In the United Kingdom, call 999 in an emergency, or Samaritans on 116 123.

Elsewhere, call your local emergency number. This website is not an emergency service and no one is monitoring it.
